# Delete your MobAtherapy 2.0 account

**Draft for review — 6 November 2026.** This page is not yet published. Please also read the [Privacy Policy](index.html).

**Application:** MobAtherapy 2.0. **Developer on Google Play:** Alexey Alykov. **Data controller:** Cardiology Research Institute, Tomsk NRMC.

## Request deletion without the application

Email [mobatherapy@mail.ru](mailto:mobatherapy@mail.ru?subject=MobAtherapy%202.0%20%E2%80%94%20account%20deletion) with the subject **MobAtherapy 2.0 — account deletion**. You do not need to install or reopen the application.

1. Send the request from your registered email address when possible.
2. State that you want your MobAtherapy 2.0 account and associated information deleted, and identify the account email.
3. If you cannot access the registered mailbox, describe the situation so that ownership can be verified. Do not send a password or unnecessary medical records.

If the email link does not open an email application, copy **mobatherapy@mail.ru** into the email service you normally use. Requests are handled within **30 days**. We will contact you if ownership information needs clarification and will confirm the result.

## Request deletion inside the application

Patients can open **Profile → Delete account and all data**, confirm the action and enter their password in the application. Do not send that password by email. Doctor and administrator accounts use the email route above.

If an error occurs, retry or contact support. Removing the application from a phone does not delete the server account.

## Information covered and remaining records

The server deletion process removes the patient profile and its stored medication plans/intake logs, diary and questionnaire records, consultation messages and photographs, covered notification/device-status entries, and the authentication account. The phone's local records are cleared after a successful server response.

Notification-delivery requests and server diagnostic logs are not all automatically cleared by the current account deletion function. Processed notification requests have an agreed retention period of **30 days after processing**, followed by deletion or irreversible de-identification; automatic cleanup is still to be implemented before this draft becomes effective. The retention and removal procedure for other controller service logs and support correspondence remains to be finalised before publication.

Firebase Crashlytics retains crash records and identifiers for 90 days before starting removal under its published retention terms. Exported medical reports held outside the application are handled separately by their recipients. Contact us about any such information; the controller will explain deletion and any applicable retention requirement. We do not promise that deleting an app account instantly erases every diagnostic or exported record.
